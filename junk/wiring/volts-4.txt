Pins on components that only have two pins are labelled using cardinal directions.

All of the ICs on this board have a 50nF capacitor attached right next to their VCC pin, which makes sense. But there's one extra 50nF cap that's sort of out in the middle of nowhere (label : C5). It's actually sort of close to the rightmost 74HC164 IC.

There's a nearby 2N4401 NPN transistor (label : Q16).
Also, a 2N4403 PNP transistor (label : Q15).

Some nearby resistors:
R123 (10k ohm)
R124 (10k ohm)
R125 (22k ohm)
R126 (10k ohm)
R127 (5k ohm)

Every measured value in the table is basically constant and instantaneous, i.e. it doesn't slowly rise. A slow rise in resistance would indicate that current is flowing through one of the large 1mF capacitors at the top of the board.

The 164 referenced in the table is the rightmost 74HC164. The leftmost 164-QH connects to the rightmost 164-A,B.
