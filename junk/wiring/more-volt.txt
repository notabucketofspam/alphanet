Both of the large capacitors seem to simply be input filters, which makes sense because I don't think that using 1000uF capacitors would make for a very accurate charge pump and/or watchdog on a digital circuit. These caps are also, like, halfway across the board from the 74HC164s.

With the Arduino completely disconnected from the led sign board, the caps stay at 5V all the time, and the voltage on the 74HC164 /CLR stays at about 0.10V, which is lower than the 0.29V I measured while the Arduino was pulsing ribbon pin 2. With the Arduino connected on all pins except for ribbon pin 2, the 74HC164 /CLR voltage is still 0.10V. With the Arduino connected to ribbon pin 2 and holding it low, the 74HC164 /CLR is about ~0.09V (basically the same as when it's just held high by the pull-up resistor).
